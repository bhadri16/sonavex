How it works

  1. 01

    Listen

    MEMS microphones pick up ambient warning sounds across the workspace.

  2. 02

    Locate

    An ESP32 microcontroller runs audio signal processing to estimate the direction the sound is coming from.

  3. 03

    Alert

    Vibration motors on a wearable band fire in the matching direction. An optional OLED display adds a visual warning.

Key features

  • Sound-direction estimation

    Multiple MEMS microphones and signal processing determine where a warning sound originates.

  • Directional vibration alerts

    A ring of vibration motors taps the side the sound is coming from, giving an intuitive spatial cue.

  • Visual warnings

    An optional OLED screen shows the sound type and direction for workers who prefer a visual reference.

  • Adjustable vibration patterns

    Different sounds map to distinct vibration rhythms, so the wearer can tell a horn from a siren by feel.

  • Lightweight and rechargeable

    Designed to be worn comfortably through a full shift and recharged overnight via USB-C.
